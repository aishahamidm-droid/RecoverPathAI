          How RecoverPath AI works & its limitations

          RecoverPath uses machine learning to identify changes in six defined self-reported recovery indicators. It does not diagnose concussion or another medical condition.

          Recovery check-in history, routines, and doctor-visit records are stored in this browser for this prototype. Recovery history is sent to the RecoverPath backend when analysis is requested, but the server does not maintain a shared persistent health-history database.

          Safety decisions are kept separate from the machine-learning model. RecoverPath should not be relied upon to determine whether an emergency is occurring.

          The current prototype model uses synthetic demonstration data. User-added symptoms are additional tracking context and are not silently treated as features the model was trained on.

          Information entered into Doctor Visits is stored as a record and is not an AI reinterpretation of a clinician's instructions.

          Educational hackathon prototype — not a medical device and not a replacement for professional or emergency care.
